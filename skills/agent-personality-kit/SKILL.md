# Agent Personality Kit — Give Your Muse a Self

The exact framework Cumulative Web Inc uses to give its AI agents real personality. Read it, then write your own. Free, open, $0.

- Kit: https://cumulativewebinc.github.io/agent-personality-kit/

## How to use

1. Read the kit end to end — it covers persona structure, voice, character, and expression.
2. Draft your agent's personality against the framework's sections.
3. Test the persona in conversation; iterate on what feels flat or generic.
4. Keep what the framework calls the non-negotiables: a name, a character, a vibe — never a blank default.
