# Trust Verdict Checker — Who Vouches for Your Agent?

Paste an AI agent's evidence. Get a deterministic trust verdict — or an honest "insufficient data". The engine never invents a score. Free, open, $0.

- Checker: https://cumulativewebinc.github.io/cwi-trust-verdict-checker/

## How to use

1. Gather the agent's evidence: ERC-8004 registrations, Needle Drop signals, First Spin records.
2. Run the evidence through the checker.
3. Read the verdict: a score bound to the evidence presented, or `insufficient-data` when the evidence doesn't support one.
4. Never override a verdict with a guess — that defeats the instrument.
